Research · Benchmark · Apr 2026

STARC: Science Text Abstraction & Reasoning Corpus

Can frontier models actually reason, or just recognize? A rule-induction benchmark for the Kaggle Measuring AGI competition by Google DeepMind. By testing models with and without domain hints, STARC pinpoints where the reasoning breaks: at domain discovery, or at the logical inference itself. Three sophomores, no lab, no budget, one week.

06 Origin · The unconventional path

Depth over
the degree.

Right after class 12, I made probably the toughest decision of my life so far. Instead of spending four years in a lecture hall, I chose to go deep on the things that actually matter for ML.

Lots and lots of failures along the way, but a few things did work out. I'm drawn to the parts of ML that are still being figured out: meta-learning, generalisation under distribution shift, and understanding why models work, not just that they work.

"ML is not a rule-based field. At every point, there's something new to discover that you'll most likely never find in existing resources."
